## Overview
This project analyzes HelloFresh revenue and cost data by collecting reports from the company 
website, converting PDF data to Excel, performing data cleaning, and running SQL analysis. The 
cleaned data is then visualized in Power BI to uncover profit drivers and cost optimization opportunities.

## Business Problem

* Inaccurate demand forecasting leads to food waste and excess production increases inventory costs
* Meal-kit business requires highly accurate forecasting to control waste and stock levels
* Profit margins are thin because operational and supply costs grow almost as fast as revenue
* Different regions show different demand and cost structures, so one common strategy does not work everywhere
* Strong competition from meal-kit brands, grocery delivery, and ready-to-eat services increases customer switching
* Customers are price sensitive, and small price changes can increase cancellations
* Sustainability pressure around packaging and delivery footprint adds additional cost and operational challenges

## Dataset

* Data was collected from HelloFresh public reports and converted from PDF format into Excel files
* Raw dataset was unstructured and required multiple format corrections
* Several fields contained inconsistent and misspelled values that were standardized
* Missing and duplicate entries were identified during cleaning
* Column names and data formats were normalized for analysis
* Cleaned dataset was prepared and loaded for SQL queries and Power BI visualization

## Research Questions & Key Findings

**Research Questions**

* What is the total revenue by year and region?
* Which region shows the highest revenue and strongest yearly growth?
* How do AEBITDA and AEBIT perform across regions and years?
* What are the AEBITDA and AEBIT margins by region?
* What is the contribution margin trend over time?
* What is the revenue and AEBITDA generated from the meal-kit business segment?
* How many total orders are placed per region each year?
* What is the average order value by region and year?
* How much is spent on procurement, cooking, and marketing expenses?
* What is the company’s working capital position?
* What is the total value of inventories and ingredients?
* How much depreciation and amortization cost is recorded yearly?

**Key Findings**

* Revenue shows clear regional differences with a few regions contributing the majority share
* Yearly revenue growth varies by region, showing uneven performance patterns
* Profitability metrics like AEBITDA and AEBIT margins differ significantly across regions
* Meal-kit segment contributes a major portion of total revenue and operating profit
* Average order value trends help explain revenue growth vs order volume changes
* Procurement, cooking, and marketing costs form a large share of operating expenses
* Inventory and ingredient values highlight working capital tied up in stock
* Depreciation and amortization represent a consistent non-cash cost component

## Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard presents revenue and cost optimization insights using multiple visualizations:

* Column charts for yearly and regional revenue comparison
* Trend lines to show revenue and margin growth over time
* Scatter charts to analyze cost vs profit relationships
* KPI cards for revenue, cost, and margin indicators
* Bar charts for regional and segment performance
* Filters and slicers for year and region-based analysis

## Final Recommendations

* Improve demand forecasting using historical revenue, order, and regional trend data to reduce overproduction and inventory waste
* Apply region-specific pricing and cost strategies since revenue, margins, and performance vary across regions
* Focus marketing spend on high-margin and high-growth regions while optimizing or reducing spend in low-return areas
* Monitor AEBITDA and contribution margins regularly to control fast-growing operational and procurement costs
* Optimize procurement and cooking expenses through supplier negotiation and better volume planning
* Use average order value and order volume metrics to design bundle offers and upsell strategies
* Control working capital by tightening inventory and ingredient stock levels based on order trends
* Track cost vs revenue trends with dashboards and set early warning KPIs for margin decline
* Balance price increases carefully with value perception to avoid customer drop due to price sensitivity
* Support sustainability goals by optimizing packaging and delivery efficiency while controlling related costs
